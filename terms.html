OpenSimply is desktop software that helps users inspect and use supported open-source GitHub projects. Support is intentionally limited to projects and execution paths OpenSimply can verify; compatibility with every GitHub repository is not promised.

Third-party open-source projects remain governed by their own licenses and terms. OpenSimply does not transfer ownership of those third-party projects.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of third-party software is lawful and appropriate for their environment. OpenSimply may refuse setup or execution when evidence, integrity, platform compatibility, or other safety requirements are not satisfied.

The founding commercial offer will provide the access period and included updates stated on the checkout page at the time of purchase. Those checkout terms control if they differ from this general page.
